# Kalimati: a fun way to learn English!

## Overview
Demo video: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_yi9sHUrg7GPp8wlGjlXvT-vjQZC3aYH/view?usp=sharing
Kalimati is a gamified English language learning platform designed to make vocabulary acquisition engaging and structured for both students and teachers. Built around a teacher-student model, the app allows educators to 
create tailored learning content while students progress through interactive games that reinforce vocabulary, spelling, and comprehension. Every user, whether teacher or student, has their own personal account, ensuring 
learning progress and content are organized and easy to manage.

At the core of Kalimati is the concept of packages: self-contained learning modules built around specific themes such as fruits, places in town, animals, or everyday objects. Each package is assigned a difficulty 
level: beginner, intermediate, or advanced, allowing teachers to tailor content to their students' proficiency and enabling learners to progress naturally as their skills improve. Students download these packages to 
access the games and vocabulary tied to that theme, making it easy to focus on one topic and level at a time.

## How It Works
Each package includes three types of games designed to reinforce vocabulary from different angles: flashcards for memorization and quick recall, sentence unscrambling for grammar and sentence structure practice, and word
matching for reinforcing word-definition associations. Together, these game types offer a well-rounded approach to vocabulary learning, combining recognition, recall, and contextual usage in a way that keeps the experience 
varied and engaging.

Teachers are given full control over content creation. Through the app, they can build new packages from scratch, adding custom words and their definitions, which are then automatically used to generate the flashcards, 
matching, and unscrambling games. Teachers can also enrich their packages with images, giving students visual context that aids memorization and makes the learning experience more intuitive, especially for beginner-level 
learners.

By combining structured, level-based content with varied game mechanics and teacher-driven customization, Kalimati aims to bridge the gap between traditional vocabulary instruction and interactive, self-paced digital 
learning, making language acquisition both effective and fun.
